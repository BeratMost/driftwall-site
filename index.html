Honest note about Wallhaven

Wallhaven is a free third-party service Driftwall doesn't own or control. If it ever changes, restricts access or shuts down, browsing it may stop working.

What you pay for is the automation, and that never depended on them — slideshows, weather rules, time-of-day rules and per-display wallpapers all run on your own folders, offline, indefinitely. Purchases go through Apple, and refunds are handled by Apple under their standard terms.

Does it upload my images anywhere?

No. Driftwall has no server and no account. Folder sources are read in place; nothing is copied or sent.

What data do you collect?

None. No analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs. See the privacy policy.

Can I use it without Wallhaven?

Yes, entirely. Add your own folders and every feature works, including offline.

Who owns the wallpapers?

Their original creators. Driftwall is a viewer — it doesn't host, alter or sell artwork, and every image links back to its source.
