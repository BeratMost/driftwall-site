Driftwall Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2024

The short version

Driftwall collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no

tracking, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. Nothing you do in the app is

sent anywhere we control, because there is nowhere we control — Driftwall has no

server.

What stays on your Mac

Everything. Specifically:

  • Your likes, collections, wallpaper history and hidden items
  • Your profile name and picture
  • Your search history
  • Your settings and slideshow rules
  • Downloaded wallpaper files
  • Any redeem code you've entered

All of it lives in ~/Library/Application Support/Driftwall and

~/Library/Caches/Driftwall. Deleting the app's folder erases all of it. None of

it is transmitted, backed up by us, or readable by us.

What leaves your Mac

Three kinds of request, all of them anonymous:

wallhaven.cc — searching and browsing sends your search terms and filters, and

downloading a wallpaper fetches the image file. Wallhaven sees your IP address, as

any website does. No account or key is used. See

wallhaven.cc/privacy-policy.

open-meteo.com — only if you turn on weather-based slideshow rules, and only

the city coordinates you chose yourself. Driftwall never requests your device

location and has no location permission. See

open-meteo.com/en/terms.

Apple — subscription purchases and restores go through the App Store. Apple

handles payment; Driftwall never sees your payment details, name or email. See

Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Driftwall requests only safe-for-work content from Wallhaven, and that filter is

not user-adjustable. Because the images are uploaded by Wallhaven's community, the

app also provides Report and Hide on every wallpaper.

Your rights

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing to request, correct or delete on

our side. To erase everything locally, delete

~/Library/Application Support/Driftwall.

Reporting content

If a wallpaper is inappropriate or infringes your copyright, use the Report

action in the app. It opens that wallpaper's page on Wallhaven, who host the image

and handle moderation and takedowns. You can also reach us at the address below and

we will pass it on.
